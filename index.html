Our Technology

We develop low-cost, easy-to-use tools that enable precise, scalable, and multiplexed analysis of glycans. By charting this underexplored landscape, researchers can uncover biomarkers and therapeutic targets previously hidden from view.

Applications & Impact

  • Biomarker discovery for early disease detection
  • Disease profiling in cancer, immune disorders, and infectious diseases
  • Identification of new therapeutic targets to accelerate drug development
  • Enhanced understanding of glycosylation changes that drive disease processes
